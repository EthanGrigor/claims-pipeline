HOMESAFE HOME INSURANCE - POLICY SUMMARY
Policy number: HH-204871
Policyholder: Sarah Mitchell
Cover: Buildings and Contents
Policy period: 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027

SECTION 1 - WHAT IS COVERED
1.1 Escape of water: sudden damage to the buildings and contents caused by water
    escaping from a fixed water installation, such as a burst pipe or tank.
1.2 Fire, smoke, storm and flood damage to the buildings and contents.
1.3 Theft of contents following forced entry to the home.

SECTION 2 - EXCESS
2.1 The standard excess is £100 per claim.
2.2 For escape of water claims, the excess is £350 per claim instead.

SECTION 3 - LIMITS
3.1 Contents: any single item is covered up to £1,500 unless listed separately.
3.2 Contents are valued on a new-for-old basis: the cost of replacing the item with a
    new one of the same type and quality, up to the limit in 3.1.

SECTION 4 - EXCLUSIONS (what is not covered)
4.1 Damage caused by water leaking gradually over time, or by wear and tear.
4.2 The cost of repairing or replacing the item that caused the water to escape
    (for example, the burst pipe itself). Damage caused by the escaping water is covered.
4.3 Any loss where the home has been unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days.

SECTION 5 - CONDITIONS
5.1 You must tell us about any claim as soon as reasonably possible, and within 30 days.
5.2 We may ask for receipts, quotes or photographs to support your claim.
